# AI Decorator

An AI that decorates your level for you.

1. Put your **API key** in this mod's settings (Anthropic by default, or any OpenAI-compatible provider).
2. In the editor, **select** the part of your layout you want decorated (or just have it on screen).
3. Hit the **AI** button next to undo/redo, describe a vibe (or pick a preset), press **Decorate!**

The AI only uses object IDs that are already in your level, in your selection, or in the *Extra Palette IDs* setting. So drop a few deco pieces you like into the selection and it'll build around them.

Everything it places is selected afterwards, put on one undo step (**Ctrl+Z** removes it all), and made **no-touch** by default so it can't break your gameplay.

You pay for your own API usage. One run is usually a few cents.
